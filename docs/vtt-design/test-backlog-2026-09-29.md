## 6. Tuesday protocol and scoring

1. **Record the run before starting.** Capture date/time/timezone, participant codes (not public names), novice/experienced classification, task order, exact source/build/working-tree hashes, feature profile, fixture manifest and browser/device/input/locale. Confirm which design version is actually implemented. A session on today's standalone editor is evidence for that editor, not proof of design v0.1.
2. **Keep the setup neutral.** Explain that the product is being tested and that participants can stop. Provide the task goal and access to the isolated account. Do not choose mechanics, silently apply a preset or give a path through the controls. Rotate the order of independent T45 tasks across participants; keep prerequisite work only where a scenario actually depends on it.
3. **Observe each core task.** Record start/end or elapsed time, outcome, mistaken interpretations, unnecessary questions, help given, abandonment, discovered default, and a permitted output/evidence reference. Retain incomplete and unsupported outcomes. Screen/audio capture is optional and requires the participant's agreement; written observations suffice, and recordings do not belong in the repository by default.
4. **Score consistently.** A successful completion produces the intended inspectable artifact/behavior without unnoticed creative defaults or moderator-chosen mechanics. If substantive assistance supplies the solution, mark `assisted`; do not count it as unassisted success. Use `completed`, `assisted`, `failed`, `abandoned`, `unsupported`, or `not_attempted`, with the reason. Exploratory supplemental tasks are reported separately.
5. **Inspect outcomes, not only clicks.** Check that the saved content matches the participant's stated intent. Record whether a chosen preset was understood; deliberate preset use is allowed, concealed defaults are not. Report manual/adjudicated steps honestly.
6. **Evaluate the required gate.** The current spec requires at least eight target users spanning novice and experienced authors and four T45 tasks each. With N participants, report completion as unassisted successful core tasks divided by **4×N**, plus per-task and experience-group counts. For exactly eight, at least **26 of 32** tasks are needed to reach 80%; zero unnoticed creative defaults and an explicit resolution for recurrent confusion are separate requirements. Unsupported/not-attempted tasks remain non-completions in the declared denominator with their reason; fewer than eight or incomplete task coverage cannot close the gate.
7. **Triage after the session.** Link each issue to a `VD-C` and/or engine T-ID, severity, reproduction, observed/expected result, owner and required recheck. Update the backlog and WP9 evidence report. Do not change previously captured outcomes to turn a later fix into a historical pass.

These scoring details operationalize the existing gate for the planned run; record any acceptance-owner adjustment before evaluating results, with its reason and effect on comparability.
