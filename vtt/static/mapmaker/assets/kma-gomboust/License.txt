ABOUT THIS SET
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

The signs symbols within this brush set were extracted from Jacques Gomboust’s 1652 map of Paris, rendered in an off-kilter isometric perspective that often gets a little weird. There’s a lot here for the discerning city builder; over six hundred brushes including: buildings, blocks, churches, cemeteries, walls, wells, houses, hospitals, and so much more. I think you’ll find the images within perfect for adding an air of authenticity to your fantasy project.

More details on this set can be found at: blog.kmalexander.com

Thanks for download this brush set and I hope you find them useful.
