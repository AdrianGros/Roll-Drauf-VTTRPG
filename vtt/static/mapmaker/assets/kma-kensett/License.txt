ABOUT THIS SET
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

This is a whimsical urban cartography brush set extracted from a delightful 19th Century map of New Haven, Connecticut, published in 1806 by engraver and tin can mogul Thomas Kensett and based on James Wadsworth's survey from 1748. Perfect for mapping a village or a small town for your fantasy project.

More details on this set and other brushes can be found at: kmalexander.com

Thanks for download this brush set and I hope you find them useful.
