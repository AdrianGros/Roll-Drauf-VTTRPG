ABOUT THIS SET
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

This is an extensive cartography brush set extracted from 日本海山潮陸圖 (Map of Sea, Mountain, Tide, and Land of Japan) that stretches horizontally and was based on the Japanese islands of Honshū, Shikoku, and Kyūshu. It's a stunning set with loads to offer, and it will help create maps that stand apart from the traditional European-influenced fantasy maps.

More details on this set and other brushes can be found at: kmalexander.com

Thanks for download this brush set and I hope you find them useful.
