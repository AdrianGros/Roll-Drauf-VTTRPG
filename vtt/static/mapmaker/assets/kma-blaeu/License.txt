ABOUT THIS SET
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

The symbols within are taken from Joan Blaeu's “Terræ Sanctæ,” a one of the forty-one copperplate wall maps found inside the Klencke Atlas, one of the largest books in the world, standing nearly six feet tall and over six feet wide when opened and weighing in at over four-hundred pounds. More details on this set can be found at: blog.kmalexander.com

Thanks for download this brush set and I hope you find them useful.

More details on this set and other brushes can be found at: kmalexander.com
